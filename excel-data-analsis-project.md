Excel Data Analysis Project

Project Overview
This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to organize, analyze, and summarize sample sales data.

Problem
Businesses need organized sales records to understand their performance and identify which products generate the most revenue.

Objective
To analyze sample sales data and summarize sales performance using Excel.

Sample Sales Data
Product         	Units Sold	       Unit Price(₦)	           Total Sales(₦)
Shoes	            10	               15000                    	150000
Bags              8	                 10000                    	80000
Shirts          	15	               5000                     	75000
Dresses         	6	                 20000	                    120000

Analysis
Total units sold: 39
Total sales revenue: ₦425,000
Best-selling product by units: Shirts
Highest-revenue product: Shoes

Excel Skills Demonstrated
Data entry and organization
Basic formulas and calculations
Sorting and filtering
Summarizing business information
Identifying sales trends

Conclusion
This sample analysis shows how Excel can help organize sales records, calculate revenue, and identify useful business insights.
