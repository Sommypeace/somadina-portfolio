Customer Support Ticket Management Project

Project Overview
This project demonstrates how to organize customer complaints and track their progress using a simple ticket register.

Problem
When customer complaints are not properly recorded, support teams may miss requests or delay responses.

Objective
To create an organized system for recording customer issues, setting priorities, and monitoring resolution.
